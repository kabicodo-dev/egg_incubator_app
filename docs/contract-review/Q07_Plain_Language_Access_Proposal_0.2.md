# SmartHatch account access — aligned simple proposal

**Revision:** 0.2 · **Prepared:** 2026-11-02
**Status:** latest user-supplied planning direction; final review pending, not
permission to change accounts, app, database, provider settings or hardware.

1. **Sign up yourself.** Create your account with email/password and verify your
   email once. Signup/verification alone does not expose incubator records.
2. **Log in normally.** Use email/password, without emailed login codes. A requested
   password-reset email is recovery, not an extra normal-login step.
3. **The team assigns the correct records once.** An authorized team member links
   your account to its correct incubator records. This assignment stays with your
   account through ordinary logout/login, reset and phone changes.
4. **No phone approval.** There is no approved-phone list or installation restriction.
   A new valid session on another phone does not need team reapproval. This does
   not approve multi-phone hardware control or allow extra physical batches.
5. **Your records stay protected.** Only your authorized account scope is accessible.
   Signing in as someone else on the same phone must not expose your local evidence.
   Local encryption/unlock is data protection, not team phone enrollment.
6. **Logout does not erase evidence or cancel a completed upload.** Protect saved
   evidence, retain original result IDs, and reconcile uncertain uploads after normal
   authorized login. A lost phone cannot recover facts that never reached the cloud.
7. **Do not disturb the occupied incubator.** No connection, Start/Stop, reset,
   reflash, settings synchronization, new session or running-batch registration is
   authorized by account signup or record assignment.
