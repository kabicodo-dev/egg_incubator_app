3.2 Temperature target, heater thresholds, safety cutoff and reset
Agreed target: 37.5 °C (99.5 °F).
Heater control: ON at ≤ 37.5 °C; OFF at ≥ 38.0 °C. There is no PID and no single “setpoint”; the effective control band is 37.5–38.0 °C. The app’s temperatureTarget: 37.5 is the low threshold, not a band midpoint.
Over-temperature cutoff: at ≥ 39.0 °C, heater OFF, humidifier OFF, circulation fan remains ON.
Reset behaviour: no latch. When the reading falls below 39.0 °C, normal control resumes. The heater re-enables only at ≤ 37.5 °C; the humidifier may re-enable immediately below 39.0 °C if humidity is below the low limit. The exhaust fan keeps its normal 30 min OFF / 30 s ON schedule; it is not forced on for cooling. There is no distinct over-temperature telemetry flag and no over-temperature buzzer.
Safety limitation: the cutoff is a software comparison on the same DHT11 that drives the heater. It is not an independent thermal fuse and cannot detect a stuck-but-plausible low reading. HW-10 clearance is not granted.
Sensor failure: after a stale timeout of 10 s, all incubator controlled outputs (heater, humidifier, circulation fan, exhaust fan) turn OFF; telemetry values become null; the buzzer alarms.

3.3 Main and lockdown humidity ranges and control rules
Main/active: 45–55 % RH.
Lockdown: 65–70 % RH.
Control: humidifier ON when RH < low limit; OFF when RH ≥ high limit; hysteresis only. There is no active dehumidifier. In lockdown the exhaust fan is disabled, so excess humidity cannot be actively removed; the chamber can only be humidified up to the high limit.
Transition: active → lockdown at the Day 19 boundary (elapsed ≥ 18 days), same instant as the turning stop.
Source-document inconsistency: the species document says both 50–55 % and 45–55 % for the active stage. The capstone table and the firmware/app use 45–55 %. Treat 45–55 % as the settled profile and correct the 50–55 % passages in the source document rather than reopening the value.

3.4 Automatic turning interval, angle/endpoints, mechanical limitations
Interval: every 4 h (EGG_TURN_INTERVAL_MS = 14 400 000), i.e. 6 turns/day.
Angle/endpoints: servo on D3; SERVO_LEFT = 0°, SERVO_RIGHT = 180°, SERVO_CENTER = 90°; 400 ms delay after the left and right moves; then returns to centre. Each turn sweeps the 0–180° span (180°) and returns to centre.
Mechanical limitations:
No servo position feedback, limit switches, or stall detection. A blocked or disconnected servo is not detected.
0°/180° may exceed the safe mechanical range of the actual turner; the firmware header explicitly says to bench-test servo limits.
The turner always returns to centre, so eggs rest at the same neutral position between turns rather than alternating rest sides.
performEggTurn() blocks the control loop for about 800 ms (two 400 ms moves) plus servo travel.
Turning is skipped during lockdown, after batch completion, or while the incubator sensor is invalid.
TURN_NOW exists in the firmware, but README.md says there is no manual “turn now” button. The app may not expose it; confirm whether that is intentional.
Agreed-document difference: the species document gives 3–5 turns/day by hand or up to 24/day automatic. The implemented 6/day is an automatic-turner choice, not an explicit species-document value. It is within the “automatic” range but should be acknowledged as an implementation decision.

3.5 Exact elapsed-time boundaries for stopping turning and changing humidity
Firmware day convention: Day = floor(elapsed_seconds / 86 400) + 1.

Boundary | Elapsed seconds | Firmware day | Behaviour
Batch start | 0 | Day 1 | Active profile; turning enabled; active humidity
Last active moment | 1 555 199 | Day 18 | Active humidity; turning still allowed subject to the 4 h schedule
Lockdown / turning stop / humidity switch | 1 555 200 (18 × 86 400) | Day 19 | Turning disabled; humidity targets become 65–70 %
Batch complete | 1 814 400 (21 × 86 400) | Day 22 (telemetry day is uncapped) | Turning and exhaust disabled; climate control continues on lockdown targets until NEW_BATCH/RESET

The firmware couples the turning stop and the humidity switch at the Day 19 start boundary.
The species document says “stop turning … on day 18” and raises humidity for Days 19–21. If that means no turning during Day 18, the firmware is one day late; if it means the last turn is at the end of Day 18, the firmware is correct. This is the main unresolved boundary definition.
After batch complete, the heater/humidifier are not automatically shut down; they continue on the lockdown targets until a new batch/reset. incubationDay() caps at 21 for the LCD/stage, but telemetry uses the uncapped elapsedDay() and can report day: 22 at completion. Confirm whether the app should display 21 or 22 at completion.

3.6 Lockdown temperature, Start/day convention, power-interruption limitations
Lockdown temperature: there is no separate Chicken lockdown temperature. The same 37.5–38.0 °C band applies throughout. The species document does not require a Chicken lockdown temperature change.
Start/day convention: a START ack is receipt only. The original Start is the separate RUNNING confirmation, after fresh sensor reads and relay-latch checks. startNewBatch() then sets elapsed_seconds = 0, which is Day 1. Day N is floor(elapsed/86 400) + 1 from that confirmed Start; it is not wall-clock time, ACK receipt time, or cloud row creation time.
Power interruption:
Batch elapsed time is saved to EEPROM every 60 s and restored at boot.
Power-off time is not counted; there is no RTC. The exact RTC chip/model is unconfirmed and RTC timekeeping has not been tested (HW-01/HW-03/HW-07).
Up to about 60 s of pre-cut elapsed time can be lost between saves; all off-time is lost.
lastEggTurn is reset to millis() at boot, so the first post-boot turn can be up to 4 h late; a turn due during the outage is skipped.
If the EEPROM batch record is invalid, boot auto-starts a new Chicken batch at Day 1 and emits batch_started.
If a batch is restored but the start record is missing/mismatched, the batch identity is marked unknown; START with a different id is rejected until an explicit NEW_BATCH/SELECT_*. An uncertain Start is never replayed automatically.
EEPROM writes are not atomic and have no CRC (A-06 limitation).
